First steps | Finish the tutorial and claim your ironman armour from Paul in Lumbridge | owned:Ironman helm; go:3229,3226,0; npc:Paul
~ On first arrival in Lumbridge:
~ Pickpocket a nearby man for an easy Lumbridge Diary task.
~ Buy a spade from the Lumbridge General Store, using 3 coins from the coin pouch you just pickpocketed.
~ Start X Marks the Spot by speaking to Veos in The Sheared Ram.
~ Dig by Bob's Axe Shop for X Marks the Spot.
~ Learn your age from Hans for an easy Lumbridge Diary task. He can be found patrolling around the outside of the castle.
~ Dig behind Lumbridge Castle for X Marks the Spot. Kill a nearby giant rat and take the raw rat meat for Druidic Ritual. Pick up the bones for an easy Varrock Diary task.
~ Bank your inventory on the 2nd floor of Lumbridge Castle, keeping only the pot and bucket, in order to have enough inventory space for the following steps.
~ Pick up nearby logs until you have at least 17 (world hop to restock) and bank these for Daddy's Home, Tree Gnome Village and Monk's Friend. A total of 65 logs are required to complete all quests.
~ Ironman armour is free to reclaim and sells for nothing: wear it, don't bank it
First steps | Complete Cook's Assistant | quest:Cook's Assistant
~ Start the quest by talking to the Cook in the kitchen on the ground floor. Pick up an additional pot from the nearby table, and a bucket from the Lumbridge Castle cellar.
~ Gather the egg, flour, and milk on the west side of the River Lum.
~ Fill a second pot of flour at the windmill for an easy Lumbridge Diary task.
~ Fill a second bucket of milk at the cow pen for Gertrude's Cat.
~ Kill a chicken for feathers for Client of Kourend and raw chicken for Druidic Ritual while in the chicken pen to pick up the egg.
~ Before returning to the Cook to complete the quest, start Sheep Shearer by talking to Fred the Farmer and gather 20 wool from the sheep in the nearby pen.
~ After quest completion, pick up a bucket from the cellar and fill it at the sink. Use it on your pot of flour to create bread dough. Use the bread dough on the range for an easy Lumbridge Diary task.
First steps | Complete Sheep Shearer, spinning the wool in Lumbridge castle | quest:Sheep Shearer
~ Spin the wool at the spinning wheel on the 1st floor of the castle.
~ Speak to Duke Horacio on the same floor to start Rune Mysteries.
~ Return to Fred the Farmer to complete Sheep Shearer.
~ After quest completion, pick an onion from the field behind Fred's house for Witch's Potion.

First steps | Complete The Restless Ghost | quest:The Restless Ghost
~ Start the quest by talking to Father Aereck in the Lumbridge church. Ensure you have not banked your air talisman given to you by Duke Horacio.
~ On the way to Father Urhney, stop by the south-eastern corner of Lumbridge Swamp near the fishing spots and speak to Abigale to start Misthalin Mystery. Complete it before continuing.
~ After speaking to Father Urhney, pick up the leather gloves, as these are required for numerous quests.
~ While at the Wizards' Tower, bring the air talisman to Archmage Sedridor in the basement for the second step of Rune Mysteries.

First steps | Clear the Stronghold of Security for its boots and 10,000 coins | varbit:2312>=1; go:3081,3421,0
~ Requires RuneScape Authenticator to be set.
~ On your way to the Barbarian Village, purchase an inventory of jugs of wine from Fortunato in Draynor Village for 1 coin each, a cheap source of food to help you pass the Stronghold.
~ At Barbarian Village climb down the entrance in between the Coal and Tin rocks. Run through dangerous rooms as fast as possible, with auto-retaliate off. This rewards players with 10,000 from just the first three floors.
~ Return to Draynor Village to use some of the coins to purchase the Chronicle, along with 10-15 teleport cards to charge it. This can be used to teleport to the Champions' Guild near Varrock for the following quests.
~ While in Draynor Village, also purchase 2 ropes from Ned for Plague City and Waterfall Quest.

First steps | Complete Gertrude's Cat | quest:Gertrude's Cat
~ Allows raising of cats. Raise a kitten to a cat to speed up Recipe for Disaster/Freeing Evil Dave and Ratcatchers. Note: Raw karambwanji is a stackable fish that can be fed to cats. Consider catching some on the next trip to Karamja.
~ On your way past the museum complete the Natural history quiz in the Varrock Museum basement.
~ Before the Lumber yard step:
~ Start Daddy's Home by talking to Marlo in the house of the Varrock Estate Agent in north-east Varrock.
~ Go to Old Man Yarlo's house south-west of Aubury's Rune Shop, talk to him and complete his tasks. Talk to him again and watch the cutscene.
~ Now search the crates in the kitchen for three waxwood logs.
~ Bring the following for the Lumber Yard step:
~ An Axe
~ Bucket of milk
~ Seasoned sardine, or create them from:
~ Raw sardine, bought from the fishing shop at Port Sarim
~ Doogle leaves, found behind Gertrude's house.
~ 3 waxwood logs
~ 10 logs and 10 sawmill coupons (wood plank) received from the Pandemonium quest
~ 3,600-4,000 (+1000 extra if not bringing coupons for the planks)
~ On the way to the Lumber Yard:
~ Talk to Regulus Cento to travel to Civitas illa Fortis and back - this will unlock other transportation methods to Varlamore. Before returning, you may also consider picking up the Black wizard robe from the 2nd floor of the pub just south of the Quetzal for an early Magic bonus.
~ Get waxwood planks, 10 regular planks, 5 bolts of cloth and around 100 bronze nails from the sawmill operator for the final step of Daddy's Home. Also buy 150 steel nails for Dragon Slayer I and Horror from the Deep.
~ Cut down a dying tree for an easy Varrock Diary task.
~ On your way back to Gertrude to finish the quest, return to Old Man Yarlo to finish Daddy's Home. Bank the steel nails beforehand, or they will be used instead of the bronze nails when constructing the furniture.
~ Save the stew reward for The Queen of Thieves, and the chocolate cake for A Tail of Two Cats.

Early members' quests | Start Enter the Abyss and take it as far as the guide says | started:Enter the Abyss
~ Start the quest by talking to the Mage of Zamorak in the Wilderness to the north of the Edgeville slayer master.
~ Talk to him again in south-eastern Varrock at the Varrock chaos temple until he gives you a scrying orb.
~ With the scrying orb, visit the rune essence mine via Aubury at Aubury's Rune Shop in Varrock to complete the first task. Take a pickaxe and collect 50 rune essence for Priest in Peril, and an additional 21 for Wanted!, The Slug Menace and Garden of Tranquillity.
~ Purchase roughly 300+ mind runes from Aubury, as well as 6 air runes, 6 water runes and 6 earth runes for Waterfall Quest. Also purchase an air staff from Zaff. Using wind strike will allow you to safespot for some early game quests like Tree Gnome Village and Fight Arena.

~ Steal from the bakery stall in the Ardougne Market until you have one cake for an easy Ardougne Diary task and two breads for Merlin's Crystal and Below Ice Mountain.

Early members' quests | Finish the Ardougne easy diary and claim Ardougne cloak 1 | varbit:4499; diary:Ardougne Easy; npc:Two-pints
~ The cloak teleports to the Ardougne Monastery as often as you like
~ Complete the remaining diary tasks in the following order:
~ Enter the Combat Training Camp north of West Ardougne.
~ Use the altar in the East Ardougne church.
~ Check what pets you have insured with Probita.
~ Sell silk to the silk trader in the Ardougne Market by offering to sell for 120 coins, then counter his bid with 60 coins.
~ Obtain a rusty sword by entering the H.A.M. Hideout north-west of Lumbridge and pickpocketing H.A.M. members. Note: There is a 1/100 chance you will require an additional sword. If you receive buttons, uncut opal, uncut jade, cowhide, or any herbs, keep them for future quests.
~ Use the Castle Wars Minigame Teleport and head to Yanille. Visit Aleck's Hunter Emporium for the task, and purchase a bird snare to be used later for an easy Desert Diary task.
~ Head to Port Khazard and give your rusty sword and 100 coins to Tindel Marchant.
~ Purchase swamp paste at the Khazard General Store for Sea Slug.
~ At the mine to the south-west collect 18 copper ore, 14 tin ore and 2 iron ore.
~ After mining the ore, board the Fishing Trawler. Once the trawler is out at sea, talk to Murphy to attempt to return to land. After capsizing, climb onto the nearby barrel to return to the mainland.
~ Complete the diary by talking to Two-pints at the Flying Horse Inn. After completion, smelt 14 copper and 14 tin ore into bronze bars for Observatory Quest, Prince Ali Rescue, One Small Favour, The Tourist Trap, Creature of Fenkenstrain, Recipe for Disaster/Freeing Pirate Pete, Shilo Village, and Mourning's End Part II's item list. The rest of the ores are for Doric's Quest.
~ Obtaining the Ardougne cloak 1 gives you the following main benefits:
~ An infinite teleport to the Ardougne Monastery with close proximity to an altar, spirit tree and fairy ring.
~ Double death runes (200) for trading in cats to civilians in West Ardougne.
~ Best early obtainable combat stats in the cape slot.
~ The antique lamp can only be used in a skill over 30 and cannot be banked. If following the guide exactly, it is recommended to put it on Prayer - you should hit level 30 at Priest in Peril.
