Skilling base | Reach 50 Firemaking at Wintertodt | skill:Firemaking 50; go:1630,3958,0; need:Cake 10
~ Bring a hammer, a knife, a tinderbox and an axe. Wintertodt crates are an iron's first big supply of seeds, herbs and ores
~ Logs from the Lumbridge castle roof, lit with a tinderbox, get you there if your Firemaking is low
Skilling base | Reach 60 Woodcutting at Wintertodt for the Woodcutting Guild | skill:Woodcutting 60; optional; go:1630,3958,0
~ Most irons stop here, at about 88 to 90 Firemaking; the guild sells a rune axe once you have 60
Skilling base | Reach 45 Thieving at the Ardougne cake stall and on master farmers | skill:Thieving 45; go:2668,3311,0
~ Master farmers in Draynor and Ardougne are your best early source of herb seeds. At 45, blackjacking in Pollnivneach pays well
Skilling base | Complete Bone Voyage to reach Fossil Island | quest:Bone Voyage
~ Kudos come from the Varrock Museum: clean finds and answer the display quizzes
Skilling base | Start birdhouse runs on Fossil Island every 50 minutes | skill:Hunter 5
~ Birdhouses give Hunter experience and a steady stream of bird nests: tree, fruit tree and herb seeds
Skilling base | Plant herb patches in Falador, Catherby and Ardougne | skill:Farming 15; go:3058,3311,0; need:Compost 3
~ Ranarr and snapdragon seeds are worth saving for Farming 32+ and 62+
Skilling base | Get a seed box from Tithe Farm | owned:Seed box; optional; go:1806,3501,0
~ Tithe Farm points also buy the herb sack and the gricoller's can
Skilling base | Get a herb sack from Tithe Farm | owned:Herb sack; optional; go:1806,3501,0
